# News monitoring

1. Read `Company/news-watchlist.md` immediately before searching.
2. Search company, product, executive, competitor, supplier, regulatory, and market terms separately.
3. Open the underlying sources for high-relevance results; do not rely only on snippets.
4. Reject duplicates, stale items presented as new, and uncertain entity matches.
5. Rank retained items as high, medium, or watch based on direct business relevance.
6. Write the sourced brief to `News/` and propose follow-up tasks separately.
