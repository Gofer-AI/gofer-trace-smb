# Company news

Bright Data research briefs are stored here as `YYYY-MM-DD-company-brief.md`.

Every reported item must include:

- headline and concise summary
- direct source URL and publisher
- published date and retrieval date
- relevance to the company
- confidence and any identity ambiguity
- a clear label for analysis or inference

No result is better than an unsourced or weakly matched result. Check recent briefs before saving to avoid duplicates.
